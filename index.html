Mission briefing

Identify what the drill brings up

Titan's atmosphere turns methane and nitrogen into a rain of organic molecules that settles on the surface. At each landing site Dragonfly collects a sample. Record its spectra, propose a structure, and the mass spectrometer will confirm or reject it.

Every instrument run draws on the battery. Whatever is left when you solve a sample is banked as flight reserve, so choose the spectrum that will tell you the most.

Instruments on board

Mass spectrometerReal. Dragonfly carries one, called DraMS. Here it checks the mass of the structure you propose.

The real payload also has a gamma-ray and neutron spectrometer, a weather and seismic package, and cameras. NMR shifts here are literature values for a room-temperature solution at 400 MHz; IR and mass spectra are simplified.
